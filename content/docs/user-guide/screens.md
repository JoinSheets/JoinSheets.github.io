JoinSheets has three screens, reached from the top nav.

## Data

Sources, joins between them, and conditions on raw fields — what comes after
`FROM`/`WHERE` in SQL. Pick which sources and fields to use, then join them
(left/right/inner/full) if there's more than one.

## Fields

What comes after `SELECT`: which columns the result table has, and how
they're computed — a field as-is, a renamed/typed field, collision
resolution between several inputs by weight, or a formula.

## Table

The result: search and filter per column, sort (click a header, or the
advanced panel for multiple levels), group with per-group aggregates, and
export to Excel or CSV.
